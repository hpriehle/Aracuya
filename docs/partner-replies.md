# Partner replies: Christmas outreach (campaign #11)

Ready answers for the replies agencies are most likely to send. Each has a short WhatsApp version and an email version. All are signed "Abina, Aracuya". Fill in `{name}` and `{ref_link}` before sending.

## Who sends what

Maddie may send these herself, as written:

- 1. Yes, interested (after "Mark yes" in Siteboard)
- 2. How and when commission is paid
- 3. How a booking is credited
- 4. Dates and availability
- 5. Group size and rooms (up to 6 guests)
- 6. Photos
- 7. Location and getting there
- 8. Can you hold dates
- 9. Discount asked (first ask only)
- 10. Not interested / stop
- 11. Catch-all holding reply

These go to Harrison (send only the catch-all, then pass it on):

- Any change to money: a different commission, a fixed fee, payment in another way or currency, paying before the stay.
- A discount pushed a second time, or any special rate or package.
- Contracts, agreements, invoices or anything to sign.
- Complaints, or a problem with a booking, a payment or a commission.
- Groups of more than 6, or any request the answers below do not cover.

Never in any reply: promise a date is free, hold dates, offer a discount, or mention Fort Apollonia (closed since Dec 2025).
